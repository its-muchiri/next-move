Tier 01 < 1000 Elo Entry Point

Starter Program

Who it's for

Absolute beginners and casual players who want to build a rock-solid foundation without getting trapped in useless opening memorization.

Target Outcome

Confident piece coordination, instant blunder awareness, and rapid promotion to 1000+ Elo.

What You'll Learn & Execute

  • Rules & Board Geometry: Intuitive sight of diagonals, open files, and the critical central four squares.
  • Essential Checkmate Patterns: Back-rank, ladder, support-mate, and queen kiss-of-death execution.
  • Opening Principles: Rapid minor piece development, center stake with pawns, and King safety before move 10.
  • Blunder Prevention Filter: A 3-step physical checklist to stop hanging pieces to simple 1-move captures.
Enroll in Starter Curriculum includes 12 video modules + live tactical drills
Tier 02 1000–1400 Elo Intermediate

Foundation Tier

Who it's for

Club and online players stuck between 1000–1400 Elo seeking an airtight calculation protocol to stop erratic play.

Target Outcome

Elimination of catastrophic calculation leaks and a dependable 1400 Elo tournament baseline.

What You'll Learn & Execute

  • Core Tactical Motifs: Systematic recognition of forks, pins, skewers, deflection, and discovered batteries.
  • King & Pawn Endgames: The square rule, key squares, opposition techniques, and converting material edges cleanly.
  • Structured Candidate Moves: Kotov's calculation branch model scaled down to reliable, rapid 2-to-3 ply checks.
  • Pacing & Clock Control: Purging impulsive blitz muscle memory and developing a healthy classical tempo.
Enroll in Foundation Includes weekly diagnostic puzzle sheets & annotated model games

Tier 03 1400–1800 Elo Advanced

Improver Program

Who it's for

Experienced club competitors and ambitious online players aiming to break through plateaus and cross into elite 1600–1800+ territory.

Target Outcome

Strategic game control, ability to outcalculate equal opponents, and sustained rating velocity.

What You'll Learn & Execute

  • Positional Prophylaxis: Karpovian piece restraint, denying counterplay before launching your own offensive operations.
  • Pawn Structure Mastery: Navigating Isolated Queen Pawn (IQP) imbalances, minority attacks, and dynamic pawn breaks.
  • Deep Candidate Trees: Calculating 5+ plies ahead in sharp middlegame structures with disciplined blunder checks.
  • Custom Repertoire Engineering: Constructing harmonious White and Black opening files suited to your tactical temperament.
Apply for Improver Tier Includes 1-on-1 monthly grandmaster sparring session
Tier 04 1800+ FIDE Master Track

Competitive Tier

Who it's for

Serious tournament contenders, FIDE title aspirants, and national competitors seeking master-grade preparation and psychological stamina.

Target Outcome

Tournament-grade poise, master-level opening conviction, and direct trajectory toward CM, FM, and NM title norms.

What You'll Learn & Execute

  • Deep Opponent Scouting: Analyzing rival PGN databases to expose stylistic vulnerabilities and craft lethal psychological traps.
  • Technical Rook & Minor Piece Endgames: Lucena, Philidor, Vancura, and nuanced opposite-colored bishop squeezes.
  • Classical Time Allocation & Stress Management: Maintaining tactical precision during sudden death and round 7 time trouble.
  • Comprehensive Post-Mortem Diagnostics: Rigorous Stockfish/Leela combined evaluation blended with human master intuition.
