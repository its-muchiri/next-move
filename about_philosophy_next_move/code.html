Founder & Head Coach

Chess isn't about memorizing moves. It's about how you reason under pressure.

Like so many dedicated players, my formative competitive years were spent memorizing thick opening tomes and chasing the latest computer novelties. Yet tournament after tournament, the painful losses kept arriving not because of theoretical deficits, but due to mental fatigue, impulsive calculation shortcuts, and panicked time-trouble scrambles.

The turning point came when I abandoned dogma and immersed myself in Soviet calculation discipline and Alexander Kotov’s candidate-move methodology. By transforming intuition into structured cognitive heuristics, I unlocked the International Grandmaster title and subsequently guided more than 450 tournament contenders to breakout rating jumps.

The undeniable truth: club competitors do not lose games because they lack engine lines. They lose because of disordered calculation trees and unchecked emotional habits. Next Move was engineered from the ground up to replace superficial study with clinical, high-stakes reasoning.
