Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our coaching protocols and curriculum.

We require 24 hours advance notice for any session rescheduling. Cancellations made with at least 24 hours notice allow full session rescheduling without any credit forfeiture. Late cancellations under 24 hours or missed appointments will count as a redeemed session credit.
No paid memberships are required on either platform. Free Lichess and Chess.com accounts are completely sufficient for our interactive study rooms and homework assignments. All master study boards, opening trees, and annotated PGN libraries will be shared directly with your account at zero additional cost.
To achieve optimal rating ROI, we recommend dedicating 2 to 4 hours per week to your structured candidate calculation drills, thematic endgame positions, and opening file revisions assigned after each coaching session.
Standard individual sessions run for exactly 60 focused minutes. For intensive tournament preparation or complex pre-game opponent preparation, extended 90-minute or 2-hour dual slots can be scheduled from your bundle credits.
We accept motivated students ranging from competitive juniors aged 8+ through dedicated adult improvers and master candidates up to 2300 FIDE. The key criteria is dedication to systematic improvement rather than initial rating.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Stripe, direct bank wire transfer, and PayPal. Invoices are automatically generated and provided in USD ($), EUR (€), or GBP (£) with zero surprise administrative fees.
